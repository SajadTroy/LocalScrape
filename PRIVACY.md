# Privacy Policy for LocalScrape

**Last Updated:** August 19, 2026

LocalScrape ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use the Extension.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage
**We do not collect, transmit, store, or log any of your personal data or page content.** 
LocalScrape is designed to operate entirely locally within your browser. 

- **No External Servers:** The Extension makes zero outbound network connections. Data extracted from webpages never leaves your local machine.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use any tracking, telemetry, or third-party analytics services to monitor your usage of the Extension.

## 2. Extension Permissions
LocalScrape requires the following permissions to function. All operations using these permissions are performed locally on your device:

- **`activeTab`**: Used strictly to allow the scraper script to interact with the current webpage only when you explicitly click the extension icon.
- **`scripting`**: Used to inject the visual highlighting styles and extraction logic into the page you have chosen to scrape.
- **`downloads`**: Used solely to save the data you have extracted (as a CSV or JSON file) directly to your computer's local Downloads folder.
